Privacy Policy - Nakshatra (நட்சத்திரா / नक्षत्र)

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Nakshatra is a panchangam and rasi palan (rashifal) app for Android. Application ID: com.nakshatra.panchang.

English

  • The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.
  • The app does not request the internet permission. Everything is calculated on your phone.
  • Names, rasis, nakshatrams and settings you enter are saved only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on, they are included in your own device backup.
  • If you allow location, the app reads your approximate location only to calculate sunrise, sunset and related timings. The location is saved on your phone and is never sent anywhere. You can instead choose a city from the list, and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.
  • Notifications are created on the phone. No account, analytics, advertising or tracking is used.
  • The app has no accounts and collects no data from anyone, including children.
  • Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
  • Contact: opulentdominionn@gmail.com
